The problem

Evaluating supplier quotations can involve bringing together information presented in different formats and levels of detail.

  • Comparing different quotation formats.
  • Reconciling prices and quantities.
  • Reviewing delivery and payment terms.
  • Identifying missing information.
  • Preparing commercial comparisons for decision-makers.

The work can be detailed. A clear, structured comparison helps teams review the information and discuss the next steps.

Founder story

Why I Built Nerqovia

Before founding Adora Innovations, I worked as an executive assistant. I had a front row seat to how businesses operate, how decisions are made, how teams coordinate and how much time is lost to repetitive manual processes.

One thing became increasingly clear to me which is many important business workflows still depend on people manually collecting, organizing and comparing information.

Procurement is one example.

Comparing supplier quotations can mean working through different document formats, reconciling prices, checking specifications, reviewing delivery and payment terms, identifying hidden costs and understanding supplier risks.

Procurement teams can spend hours building comparison tables from scratch, often repeating the same work with every purchasing exercise.

I became interested in how AI could help make these workflows more structured, efficient and easier to evaluate.

That thinking led to Nerqovia.

I am building Nerqovia to help procurement teams organize supplier quotations, identify commercial differences and gaps, and produce structured intelligence that supports better-informed purchasing decisions.

My perspective comes from observing business operations from the inside, listening to professionals who experience these challenges and building technology around problems that deserve better solutions.

Developed with insights and validation from conversations with procurement professionals and business leaders.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about Nerqovia

What is Nerqovia?+

Nerqovia is a commercial intelligence product that helps procurement teams organize and compare supplier quotations, identify commercial differences and missing information, and prepare structured findings for purchasing decisions.

Who is Nerqovia designed for?+

Nerqovia is being developed for procurement professionals, supply chain teams, operations leaders and business owners who evaluate supplier quotations. Its initial market focus includes FMCG, food processing and packaging businesses.

How does Nerqovia work?+

Users upload supported supplier quotation documents. Nerqovia extracts and organizes available information, compares supplier offers and generates a Commercial Intelligence Brief for review.

What does Nerqovia help us identify?+

Depending on the information supplied, it can help highlight price differences, specification differences, delivery and payment terms, missing information, potential commercial risks and questions requiring supplier clarification.

Does Nerqovia choose the winning supplier?+

No. Nerqovia supports the evaluation process. Your procurement team remains responsible for assessing the findings, applying company requirements and making the final supplier decision.

How does Nerqovia handle confidential supplier quotations?+

Supplier quotations may contain sensitive commercial information. Before using real company documents, customers should understand the applicable data-handling, access and retention arrangements. Pilot evaluations should use agreed documents and appropriate permissions. Contact us for the current data-handling details.

Do we need to replace our existing procurement software?+

Nerqovia is currently focused on supplier quotation evaluation. It is not positioned as a replacement for your ERP or complete procurement management system.

How can my company evaluate Nerqovia?+

Companies interested in evaluating Nerqovia can submit a pilot enquiry. We will discuss their quotation evaluation workflow, the suitability of the use case and the arrangements for a controlled evaluation.
